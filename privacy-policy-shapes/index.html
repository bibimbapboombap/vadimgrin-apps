Privacy Policy — Just Shapes

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Just Shapes (“the app”) is a puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect any personal data. Since version 1.01.00 it sends anonymous usage statistics, described below, which are not linked to you.

Information I collect

No personal data. The app does not ask you to create an account or provide a name, email address, or any other personal information. There is no sign-up and no login.

The app does not:

  • collect or transmit personal data;
  • use advertising, or track you across other apps or websites;
  • access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, calendar, or health data;
  • send anything over the network other than the anonymous usage statistics described below.

Anonymous usage statistics

Just Shapes sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck (telemetrydeck.com), a privacy-focused analytics service, so that I can see how the game is played and make it better — for example, which levels are started, won or lost and how long they take, which boosters and settings are used, and how far the tutorial is followed. Each report also includes the app version, the device model, the iOS version, and the language and region set on the device.

These statistics contain no personal information. They are not linked to you, to an account, or to your activity in other apps or on websites. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses, and it receives only a scrambled (salted and hashed) form of a random identifier created for this installation, which cannot be traced back to you or to your device. The statistics are not used for advertising or tracking, and they are not sold or shared with anyone else.

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress and settings on your device so they can be restored the next time you play. This is stored using Apple’s standard on-device storage, and it:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not visible or accessible to me;
  • is permanently removed when you delete the app.

Third parties

Apart from TelemetryDeck, described above, the app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services. Nothing is sold, rented, traded, or shared with anyone else.

Your rights

The app collects no personal data, and the usage statistics cannot be traced back to you, so there is nothing for me to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You remain in full control: deleting the app removes everything it stores on your device.

Information Apple may collect

When you download the app from the App Store, Apple may collect information such as download and crash statistics under its own policies. If you have chosen to share analytics with developers, I may see aggregated, anonymised diagnostics through Apple’s tools. That information does not identify you personally and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, not this one.

Children’s privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The anonymous usage statistics described above contain no personal information and are not used to identify or profile anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact me.
